\begin{abstract}
Let $X$ be a smooth complex projective variety of dimension $n$ and let $L$ be an ample line bundle. We prove that $K_X+mL$ is globally generated for every integer $m\geq n+1$. The bound is sharp on projective space. The proof constructs a point-centered order by finite-degree minimization and uses it to lift a nonzero value of the adjoint bundle.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

Fujita's freeness conjecture asks when an ample twist of the canonical bundle is globally generated \cite{fujita1987}. We write line bundles additively.

\begin{maintheorem}\label{thm:main}
Let $X$ be a smooth connected projective variety over $\mathbb{C}$ of dimension $n\geq 1$, and let $L$ be an ample line bundle on $X$. Then
\[
                    K_X+(n+1)L
\]
is generated by its global sections.
\end{maintheorem}

This exposition follows the proof in \cite{OpenAIMathSource}. Applying the theorem componentwise also covers disconnected varieties. For $X=\mathbb{P}^n$ and $L=\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^n}(1)$ one has $K_X+(n+1)L\simeq\mathcal{O}_X$ and $K_X+nL\simeq\mathcal{O}_X(-1)$, so the exponent is sharp. The assertion in dimension zero holds because a smooth projective zero-dimensional complex variety is a finite disjoint union of points.

For any integer $r\geq0$, apply the theorem to $X\times\mathbb{P}^r$ with $L\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^r}(1)$. Its adjoint bundle is
\[
 (K_X+(n+r+1)L)\boxtimes\mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^r}(n).
\]
Restriction to $X\times\{z\}$ gives global generation for every twist $m=n+r+1$.

The low-dimensional cases follow from Riemann--Roch in dimension one, Reider in dimension two, and the work of Ein--Lazarsfeld, Kawamata, and Ye--Zhu in dimensions three through five \cite{reider1988,einlazarsfeld1993,kawamata1997,yezhu2020}. In arbitrary dimension, the effective results of Angehrn--Siu and Ghidelli--Lacini give quadratic and $n(\log\log n+2.34)$ bounds, respectively \cite{angehrnsiu1995}\cite[Theorem~1.1]{ghidellilacini2024}. The present argument uses a finite-degree minimum to control the dimension of a center over a prescribed point.

Fix $x\in X$ and suppose that every adjoint section vanishes at $x$. Under this hypothesis an equality-case argument forces the asymptotic mean order of a basis adapted to $\ord_x$, divided by its degree, to exceed $n/(n+1)$ (\cref{sec:min-ordinary}). Choose $t<n+1$ close to $n+1$. For large degrees $m$, we minimize
\[
 \frac{1}{h^0(X,mL)}
 \sum_i\exp\!\left(\frac{A(v)}{t}-\frac{v(s_i)}{m}\right)
\]
over bases $(s_i)$ of $H^0(X,mL)$ and weighted monomial orders $v$ whose centers contain $x$. Here $A(v)$ is the log discrepancy. The strict mean-order bound makes the minimum less than $1$ by varying the scale of $\ord_x$. Closed threshold loci on a flag variety and retraction to a log resolution give attainment (\cref{sec:min-exponential}).

Variations tangent to a positive-dimensional center would force a weighted first moment smaller than the one supplied by the restricted sections. The lower estimate is uniform over the integral subvarieties of each dimension, which allows the center to vary with $m$ (\cref{sec:centers}). A point-centered minimizer remains. Its supporting functional yields rational divisor data with an isolated discrepancy-equality locus over $x$. Kawamata--Viehweg vanishing lifts a section nonzero there, and descent gives a section of the adjoint bundle nonzero at $x$ (\cref{sec:isolation}).
