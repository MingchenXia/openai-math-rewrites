\begin{abstract}
We prove that a connected bounded semialgebraic open subset of a complex affine variety is smooth and biholomorphic to a bounded symmetric domain if it admits a properly discontinuous cocompact action by biholomorphisms. This answers the bounded-domain question of Koll\'ar and Pardon.
\end{abstract}

\maketitle

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

Fix a closed affine embedding of a reduced complex algebraic set. Semialgebraicity and boundedness refer to this embedding, and openness refers to the complex topology. A bounded symmetric domain is a bounded connected open set in a complex vector space with a holomorphic involution at each point that fixes it in isolation.

\begin{maintheorem}\label{thm:main}
Let $U$ be a nonempty connected semialgebraic bounded open subset of a complex affine variety. Suppose that a group $\Gamma$ of biholomorphisms acts properly discontinuously on $U$ and that $U/\Gamma$ is compact. Then $U$ is smooth and is biholomorphic to a bounded symmetric domain.
\end{maintheorem}

We regard $\Gamma$ as discrete in the properness condition. In particular, stabilizers can be finite, and a point is allowed as a zero-dimensional domain. The theorem answers Question~25 of Koll\'ar and Pardon \cite[arXiv version~2, Question~25]{KP2012}. This exposition follows \cite{OpenAIMathSource}.

Wong and Rosay identify the ball from an orbit accumulating at a strongly pseudoconvex boundary point \cite{Wong1977,Rosay1979}. Vey's theorem instead identifies the bounded symmetric domains among divisible generalized Siegel domains \cite[Theorem~1 and the following paragraph]{Vey1970}. Our proof reaches such a domain from semialgebraic boundary data.

First, a polynomial-weighted maximum localizes automorphism orbits near a smooth point of the ambient affine variety. Cocompactness then gives smoothness of $U$ and properness of the chain-of-discs distance (\cref{sec:geometry}). In positive dimension, a semialgebraic boundary stratum supplies jointly controlled limits of normal offsets. Independent holomorphic supports bound all complex-normal directions, while attached analytic discs give interior balls of controlled radius at the rescaled scale (\cref{sec:preparation,sec:noncollapse}).

Rescaling by $t^{-1/2}$ tangentially and $t^{-1}$ normally gives a model
\[
 \{(z,w)\in\mathbb{C}^m\times\mathbb{C}^k:
   \operatorname{Im} w-H(z,z)\in C\},
\]
where $m+k=\dim U$, $k\geq1$, $C\subset\mathbb{R}^k$ is a connected open cone, and $H$ is a vector of Hermitian forms. Both interior and excluded-point estimates are needed to take limits of the normalized maps and their inverses. They give a biholomorphism with the entire model (\cref{sec:scaling}). The support inequalities give a bounded realization, and the transferred compact quotient allows Vey's theorem to be applied (\cref{sec:symmetry}).
